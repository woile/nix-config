In the mythology of the Andean civilizations of South America, the [amaru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amaru_(mythology)) is a mythical serpent or dragon.
It is an animal that traverses worlds and is capable of transcending boundaries to and from the spiritual realm of the underworld.

Like [amaru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amaru_(mythology)), this machine connects the cloud world to the physical world.
